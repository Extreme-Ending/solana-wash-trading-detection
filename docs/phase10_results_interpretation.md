# Phase 10 results interpretation and figure audit

## Evidence boundary

All figures and tables are generated by `scripts/generate_phase9_figures.py` from the preprocessing, feature, experimental-model and evaluation reports whose hashes appear in `reports/phase9/figure_manifest.json`. The generator fails if labels are claimed, confirmed outcomes are reported, row counts fail to reconcile, the feature count differs from 29 or artifact fingerprints disagree.

## Figure audit

### Figure 1 — Chronological wallet cohorts

- **File:** `reports/phase9/figure_01_temporal_cohorts.svg`
- **Axes/units:** scoring cutoff on the horizontal axis; wallet snapshot count (wallets) on the vertical axis.
- **Source:** feature report cutoff counts and temporal-overlap evaluation.
- **Caption:** *Wallet snapshots available at each fixed UTC scoring cutoff, separated conceptually into recurring and newly observed wallets. All 1,754 September reference wallets recur later; 176 wallets enter by October and 382 more by December.*
- **Interpretation:** the design is chronological but later cumulative snapshots are not wallet-independent. New entrants are identity-disjoint from the reference but have shorter observable histories.

### Figure 2 — Isolation Forest score summaries

- **File:** `reports/phase9/figure_02_isolation_score_summaries.svg`
- **Axes/units:** cohort on the horizontal axis; unitless model-specific Isolation Forest anomaly score on the vertical axis.
- **Source:** evaluation score summaries.
- **Caption:** *Distribution summaries for Isolation Forest scores across the reference and later cohorts. Higher values indicate greater model-specific unusualness relative to the fitted reference; values are not probabilities or cross-model comparable units.*
- **Interpretation:** later/new-wallet scores differ, but cohort, coverage and activity-duration differences prevent a causal or fraud interpretation.

### Figure 3 — Holdout rank agreement

- **File:** `reports/phase9/figure_03_holdout_rank_agreement.svg`
- **Axes/units:** detector names on both axes; cell colour/text is Spearman rank correlation in `[-1, 1]`.
- **Source:** December holdout rank comparison.
- **Caption:** *Pairwise Spearman correlation between detector rankings on 2,312 December wallet snapshots: IF–LOF `0.0488`, IF–MAD `0.8662`, and LOF–MAD `-0.0187`.*
- **Interpretation:** Isolation Forest and robust MAD produce broadly aligned rankings; LOF differs materially. Correlation measures agreement, not correctness.

### Figure 4 — Isolation Forest parameter sensitivity

- **File:** `reports/phase9/figure_04_parameter_sensitivity.svg`
- **Axes/units:** tested Isolation Forest configuration on the horizontal axis; Spearman correlation with the declared validation ranking on the vertical axis.
- **Source:** October validation-only sensitivity experiment.
- **Caption:** *Validation ranking stability under the predeclared estimator/sample-size alternatives. Correlations range from `0.9923` to `0.9980`.*
- **Interpretation:** rankings were stable within this bounded setting range. This is not proof of optimal hyperparameters or effectiveness against fraud, and the holdout was not used for the sensitivity choice.

### Figure 5 — Coverage and score context

- **File:** `reports/phase9/figure_05_coverage_score_context.svg`
- **Axes/units:** declared descriptive stratum on the horizontal axis; median unitless Isolation Forest score on the vertical axis.
- **Source:** holdout coverage/volume stratification.
- **Caption:** *Median holdout Isolation Forest scores for incomplete-history, single/multi-event and high-volume descriptive strata.*
- **Interpretation:** score distributions are associated with observable history and volume. The bars are not per-feature contributions, SHAP values, effects or evidence that high-volume activity is improper.

## Fingerprint audit

The manifest binds the generated package to the current evaluation, cleaning report, feature report and model manifest. Phase 9 generated the package twice with byte-identical results. Phase 10 must regenerate it again during regression testing and compare it with the tracked package before declaring readiness.

## Supported findings

- 10,800 original records; 10,798 retained; two quarantined.
- 5,996 snapshots with 29 registered point-in-time features.
- stable Isolation Forest validation ranks within the tested alternatives.
- substantial detector disagreement, especially involving LOF.
- limited intersection across top review budgets.
- repeated-wallet dependence and later-entrant coverage limitations.
- reproducible local outputs under the pinned environment.

## Unsupported findings

The figures do not support precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, calibration, a fraud rate, a wash-trader count, a probability of misconduct, causal feature importance or generalisation to the Solana population.
