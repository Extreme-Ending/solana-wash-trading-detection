AI assistance did not supply ground-truth labels, conduct an independent human fraud investigation, or resolve dataset provenance and licensing.
>
> The final dissertation and presentation require the student's own critical review, rewriting and verification. Any errors remaining in the submitted work are the student's responsibility.
