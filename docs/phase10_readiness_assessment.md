## 4. Research alignment recommendation

Recommended working title:

**Unsupervised Wallet Anomaly Detection in Solana DEX Trading: A Reproducible Risk Monitoring Service**

The recommended unit is `(wallet, as_of)` and the objective is relative anomaly ranking from strictly prior events. Appropriate evaluation is rank stability, method agreement, top-budget overlap, parameter sensitivity, feature shift and coverage diagnostics. Outcome metrics remain prohibited.

The student's earlier statement that the direction was accepted is noted, but a primary written amendment/approval record is absent. The new proposed amendment is therefore a reconciliation draft, not a substitute for that record.
