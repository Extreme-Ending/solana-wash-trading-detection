# Phase 10 scholarly reference audit

## Audit method

Author, year, title, venue, pagination/article number and DOI or stable URL were checked against publisher pages, DOI records, authors' institutional copies or established bibliographic records. No quotations were imported. “Relevance” records why the source is cited; it is not an assertion that its results replicate on this dataset.

## Excluded or non-scholarly sources

- The lecturer's GitHub repository is cited only for course-method alignment, not as peer-reviewed scholarship.
- Search-result summaries, encyclopaedia pages and AI-generated paper summaries were not used as references.
- Recent papers with unclear venue quality or unverifiable metadata were excluded even when topically similar.
- Solana documentation is used for RPC/provenance mechanics, not as scholarly anomaly-detection evidence.
- The project's own reports are empirical evidence, not literature.

## DOI note for LOF

Bibliographic systems expose both an ACM proceedings DOI and a legacy ACM record. The reference list uses `10.1145/342009.335388`, the proceedings citation reported by DBLP/ACM-linked records. The paper identity, authors, title, year and pages are consistent across the records.

## Audit limitations

The repository has no institutional library export or supervisor-approved bibliography. The student must confirm house style, required retrieval-date rules and whether additional Solana-specific literature is expected. APA formatting here is a checked working bibliography, not a claim of final departmental acceptance.
